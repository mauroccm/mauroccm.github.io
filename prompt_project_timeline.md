- Jan–Feb 2026 - Testing different modeling approaches for yellow fever risk and its association with temperature. What is the best way to use temperature as a predictor (driver) of yellow fever risk? I tested three approaches, and the occurrence of 7 or more consecutive days with a minimum temperature above 17°C between April and September showed the strongest association.
- Mar 2026 - I was looking for studies on "climate hesitancy" and found the work of Fujita et al. (2024), which identifies climate change as a factor responsible for shifts in the dengue serotype profile in Brazil.
